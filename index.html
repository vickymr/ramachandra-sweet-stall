How to Place Your Order

Simple 4-step process to get fresh sweets delivered to your door

1

Browse & Add to Cart

Explore our sweets and savouries. Choose your size (250g, 500g, 1kg) and add to cart.

2

Provide Delivery Details

Click checkout inside your basket, fill in your name, WhatsApp number, and delivery address.

3

Get PDF Receipt

Confirm details and place order. Your official PDF order receipt will download automatically.

4

WhatsApp Confirmation

Click "Open WhatsApp to Confirm" to send the order details text directly to the store owner.
